CyberGuard // Privacy

Your privacy, in plain words

CyberGuard is a free educational security portal. There are no accounts and no ads. This page explains exactly what happens to the information you type or click while using it.

Last updated: 17 October 2026 Applies to this website only

The short version

  • Most tools run inside your browser. What you type stays on your device.
  • A few tools ask a public service for real data (for example a breach database). That request leaves your device, and the table below lists exactly what is sent.
  • Your password is never sent anywhere. The password checker sends only the first 5 characters of a scrambled (hashed) version.
  • Posts on the Live Feedback wall are public. Do not put private details in them.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, use tracking cookies, or ask for your microphone or camera.
01 · Stays with you

What is processed only on your device

These features work entirely in your browser and send nothing to any server:

  • Password strength analysis, password generator and the AES text cipher
  • JWT decoder, Base64, URL encoder/decoder and text hash generator
  • CIDR calculator, IBAN validator, Wi-Fi protocol check, file-extension risk check
  • SQL-injection and XSS pattern checkers (your text is analysed locally)
  • File hashing and QR image reading: the file or image is read locally and never uploaded
  • All six games, including the Scam Call Lab. The scammer's voice uses your browser's text-to-speech (only the scripted game lines are spoken, and some browsers use their vendor's online voices). No microphone is used.

03 · Public

The Live Feedback wall is public

When you submit feedback, the following is saved in a Google Firebase Realtime Database and shown on the public wall for everyone to read:

  • the name and role / city you typed, your rating, category, mood and message
  • the time of the post and the reaction counts

Use a nickname and keep personal information, phone numbers, CNIC details, passwords and private stories out of your post. To have a post removed, contact us using the details below and tell us the name and message you used.

04 · Support

WhatsApp support and the incident form

The support buttons open WhatsApp with a prepared message, so you decide whether to send it. After you send it, your message and number are handled by WhatsApp and by our support desk under WhatsApp's own policy.

The incident-report form on the Contact page prepares a ticket number and a WhatsApp message on your device. Nothing you type in that form is uploaded to a CyberGuard server. Avoid including passwords, full card numbers or OTPs in any report.

06 · Control

What we do not do, and your choices

We do not

  • Sell or rent your information.
  • Show advertising or build advertising profiles.
  • Require an account, login or email address.
  • Use your microphone or camera.
  • Upload your files, images or passwords.

You can

  • Clear all local data with the button above, or via your browser settings.
  • Ask us to remove a post you made on the feedback wall.
  • Block third-party requests using your browser or a content blocker. Some tools will then show an "unreachable" message instead of live data.
  • Use the site in a private window so nothing is kept after you close it.
07 · More

Security, children and changes

Hosting logs. Like any website, the company that hosts CyberGuard may keep standard server logs (IP address, time and page requested) for security and reliability. We do not use them to identify visitors.

Children. The site is made for general safety education and is not designed to collect information from children. Young users should use it with a parent or teacher, and should not post on the public wall.

Changes. If the site starts using new services or storing new information, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change.

Contact. Questions or removal requests: WhatsApp 0326-2116352, or use the Contact Us page from the main menu.

This page describes how the site currently behaves. It is an explanation for visitors and not legal advice.
