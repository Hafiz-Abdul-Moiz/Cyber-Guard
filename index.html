DEFENSE KNOWLEDGE BASE

Know The Scam Before The Scam Knows You

Neeche common scam patterns diye gaye hain. Har card par hover karke attack pattern aur defense rule dekho.

💸 WALLET CRITICAL

Fake Payment / Accidental Transfer

Scammer fake payment SMS, edited receipt ya fake wallet notification bhej kar refund maangta hai.

FAKE SMS → PRESSURE → REFUND
🛡️ DEFENSE: SMS screenshot par trust mat karo. Official wallet app khud open karke balance aur transaction verify karo.
📱 SOCIAL HIGH

Fake Shopping & Marketplace Scam

Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp ya marketplace par unbelievable discount dekar advance payment li jati hai.

CHEAP OFFER → ADVANCE → GHOST
🛡️ DEFENSE: Seller ko independently verify karo. Unknown seller ko advance payment bhejne se pehle transaction risk check karo.
🎣 PHISHING CRITICAL

Fake Login / Account Verification Link

"Account blocked", "verify now", "security alert" jese messages fake login page tak le ja sakte hain.

URGENT MSG → FAKE SITE → CREDENTIALS
🛡️ DEFENSE: Message ke link par click na karo. Official website ya official app manually open karo.
🤖 AI FRAUD CRITICAL

AI Voice Clone Family Emergency

AI-generated voice kisi family member ki awaaz jesi lag sakti hai aur attacker emergency ka bahana bana sakta hai.

CLONED VOICE → PANIC → MONEY
🛡️ DEFENSE: Caller ki awaaz ko identity proof mat samjho. Trusted number par khud call karke verify karo.
📲 MOBILE CRITICAL

Malicious APK / WhatsApp App Scam

Fake bank, reward, government scheme ya delivery app ke naam par malicious APK bheji ja sakti hai.

APK → PERMISSION → DATA THEFT
🛡️ DEFENSE: Unknown APK install mat karo. Apps official store se hi install karo aur permissions review karo.
🏦 IMPERSONATION HIGH

Fake Bank / Support Agent

Caller khud ko bank representative, support agent ya verification officer bata kar sensitive information maang sakta hai.

CALL → TRUST → OTP
🛡️ DEFENSE: OTP, PIN, CVV aur passwords kisi caller ko share mat karo. Official number khud find karke contact karo.
🔳 QR FRAUD HIGH

QR Code Receive-Money Trap

Scammer keh sakta hai ke "money receive karne ke liye QR scan karo", jabke QR payment action initiate kar sakta hai.

QR → APPROVE → DEBIT
🛡️ DEFENSE: Receive-money ke naam par PIN ya payment approval kabhi blindly enter mat karo.
💼 JOB SCAM HIGH

Fake Online Job / Task Scam

Fake jobs, like-and-subscribe tasks ya freelance offers ke naam par registration, security ya withdrawal fee maangi ja sakti hai.

JOB OFFER → FEE → NO JOB
🛡️ DEFENSE: Job lene ke liye paisa maanga jaye to company, domain aur offer ko independently verify karo.
📈 INVESTMENT CRITICAL

AI Trading / Guaranteed Profit Scam

Fake AI trading bots, crypto groups aur influencer testimonials guaranteed profit ka illusion create karte hain.

PROFIT → DEPOSIT → LOCKED
🛡️ DEFENSE: Guaranteed returns par trust mat karo. Investment platform aur registration independently verify karo.
🎁 GIVEAWAY MEDIUM

Fake Giveaway / Prize Link

"You won iPhone", "free cash", "lucky draw winner" jese posts users ko fake websites ya surveys par bhejte hain.

PRIZE → LINK → DATA
🛡️ DEFENSE: Prize claim karne ke liye password, OTP, card details ya suspicious fee maangi jaye to stop.
📡 SIM SWAP CRITICAL

SIM Swap / OTP Interception

Attacker SIM replacement ya port-out process abuse karke SMS-based verification codes intercept karne ki koshish kar sakta hai.

SIM → OTP → ACCOUNT
🛡️ DEFENSE: Unexpectedly mobile service band ho jaye to telecom provider se immediately verify karo.
📦 DELIVERY HIGH

Fake Parcel / Customs Fee Scam

Fake courier SMS ya email parcel clearance, customs fee ya delivery payment ke naam par suspicious link bhej sakta hai.

PARCEL → FEE → PHISHING
🛡️ DEFENSE: Courier ka official app/site manually open karke tracking aur payment details verify karo.

🚨 Emergency Incident Response Protocol

Follow these 15 critical emergency steps immediately if you are hacked, scammed, or experiencing a cyber incident:

  1. Network Isolation: Disconnect Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and Mobile Data instantly to stop data exfiltration.
  2. Bank & Wallet Freeze: Call 3737 (EasyPaisa), 4444 (JazzCash), or your Bank Helpline to freeze accounts immediately.
  3. Session Invalidation: Go to security settings and click "Log Out of All Devices" on Email, WhatsApp, and Social accounts.
  4. Credential Rotation: Change master passwords for Email and Banking apps from a separate, secure device.
  5. DO NOT Share OTP/PIN: Never disclose OTPs or PINs to anyone, even if they claim to be Bank Officers or Police.
  6. WhatsApp Screen Sharing Warning: Immediately terminate screen sharing on WhatsApp/calls if requested by unknown callers.
  7. Malicious App Removal: Uninstall suspicious `.apk` files or remote apps like AnyDesk and TeamViewer in Safe Mode.
  8. Cybercrime Reporting: File an official complaint at complaint.fia.gov.pk (Pakistan) or cybercrime.gov.in (India).
  9. Financial Dispute Logging: Note down transaction IDs and report fraudulent transactions within 24 hours for chargeback.
  10. SIM Card Security: Contact your telecom carrier immediately if your SIM signal drops unexpectedly to prevent SIM Swap fraud.
  11. ATM Skimmer Check: Never insert your card or enter PIN if the ATM card slot feels loose, wobbly, or shows glue marks.
  12. QR Code Scanning Rule: Remember that scanning a QR code and entering your PIN always DEDUCTS money, never credits it.
  13. Family Emergency Verification: Always hang up and call family members directly to verify AI voice clone emergency calls.
  14. Evidence Preservation: Take clear screenshots of chat logs, caller numbers, and transaction receipts before deleting anything.
  15. Factory Data Reset: Perform a complete Factory Reset if deep spyware or malware persists after app uninstallation.
